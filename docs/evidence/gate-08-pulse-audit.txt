CYBERPUN-17-14-t4 (PR 4) -- gate 8 audit trace: pulse mechanic
================================================================

Scope: "the pulse works and is visible -- a screenshot at the moment of
the pulse, raccoons mid-shove, the ring drawn, a raccoon pinned against a
building" (gate 8).

Method
------
Re-read `PulseAbility` (CYBERPUN-17-10 PR 1), `PulseRingNode` (PR 3) and
`GameScene.applyPulseTrigger(raccoons:)`/`applyPulseHit(_:)` (the
production scene wiring) against gate 8's four named visual claims:

1. The ring drawn: `applyPulseTrigger` calls `pulseRing.play(radiusTiles:
   at:)` on every successful trigger -- even when `hits` is empty, since
   the pulse still fires visually with nobody in range
   (`PulseAbility.Result.hits`'s own doc comment). Covered by
   `PulseSceneWiringTests
   .test_pressingThePulseButton_firesTheAbility_evenWithNoRaccoonsInRange`
   and `.test_applyPulseTrigger_whenOffCooldown_pushesAndDamagesRaccoons_andSpawnsTheRing_inOneCall`
   (asserts the ring's own animation action is actually running, not just
   unhidden). No offender found.

2. Raccoons mid-shove: `PulseAbility.trigger` computes each in-radius
   living raccoon's `pushTarget` (along the ray from the player through the
   raccoon's original position, at radius + a small overshoot) and
   `applyPulseHit` applies the resolved position to the live `RaccoonNode`
   in the same call that plays the ring -- push and ring are one atomic
   scene update, so a captured frame at trigger time genuinely shows both
   together. Covered directly by
   `PulseSceneWiringTests.test_applyPulseTrigger_whenOffCooldown_...`
   (asserts the raccoon's on-screen position reflects the pushed tile
   position in the same call that plays the ring). No offender found.

3. The ring drawn at the moment of pulse specifically (not delayed/stale):
   `pulseRing.play(...)` runs synchronously inside `applyPulseTrigger`,
   called directly from `handlePulsePress()`, which is the HUD pulse
   button's production `onPress` target
   (`PulseSceneWiringTests.test_aTouchAtTheVisibleAbilityButtonsSlot_routesToIt_andFiresTheAbility`
   drives this through the real touch-dispatch path, not just a direct
   method call). No offender found.

4. A raccoon pinned against a building: `PulseAbility.trigger` receives
   `obstructions` and pushes each candidate through
   `CollisionResolver.resolve`, scoring `wasCrushed` when the *resolved*
   position still lands inside the pulse radius (a building blocked the
   push). Critically, `GameScene.applyPulseTrigger` passes
   `groundPlane?.residentObstructions` -- the same real, currently-streamed
   building footprints `RaccoonSpawnDirector`'s own steering already
   avoids, not an empty/synthetic list -- so a raccoon pinned against a
   *real* building is reachable in a live run, not only under a
   hand-built fixture. `wasCrushed`'s pure logic is exhaustively covered by
   `PulseAbilityTests` (clear push vs. crushed vs. deflected-but-still-clear,
   across multiple height classes -- crush detection is footprint-only and
   must not vary with a building's rendered height). No offender found.

Two items already recorded as outstanding by earlier stories, unchanged by
this audit and not reopened here (both from the `CYBERPUN-17-10` entry in
AGENT.md/CLAUDE.md): the level-6 pulse radius bonus ships *compounding*
(1.5625x, not additive 1.5x) pending a human tuning call, and the crash the
runtime probe twice reported near this ability remains genuinely
unidentified -- both are named there, not re-litigated here, since this
PR's scope is the pulse's *visual* gate-8 claims, all four of which hold.

Finding: no offender
---------------------
All four of gate 8's named visual claims hold, confirmed by the existing
`PulseAbilityTests`/`PulseSceneWiringTests`/`PulseRingNodeTests` suites and
by direct re-reading of the scene-wiring call chain (`handlePulsePress`
-> `applyPulseTrigger` -> `applyPulseHit`/`pulseRing.play`). No production
code change was needed for gate 8.

What this audit does NOT evidence
-----------------------------------
This is a code-level/in-suite check -- it is not a recording of a real
simulator run. A human (or the platform's runtime-probe pipeline) still
needs to capture `gate-08-pulse.png`: a screenshot at the moment of a real
pulse press showing the ring drawn, raccoons visibly mid-shove, and at
least one raccoon pinned against a building.
